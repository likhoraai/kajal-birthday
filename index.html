A Letter for Kajal

Some things are too big for a shirt. Tap the envelope.

Dear Kajal,

First things first — happy birthday to us! I still cannot decide whether the calendar made a mistake giving us the same day, or whether it was simply being generous. Either way, I would not change it for anything.

I keep thinking about farewell day. Everyone was scribbling goodbye messages on each other's shirts, and when my turn came, I did not have to think at all. Two words were enough: FRIEND FOREVER. I wrote them because they are true — shirts fade and schools end, but that part does not.

Thank you for being the friend who makes ordinary days lighter — for the corridor laughs, for the support that never needs asking, and for sharing my birthday so I never have to celebrate it alone. (Also, fair warning: I will be claiming the bigger cake slice this year too.)

I wish you a year as bright as you make everyone else's days. Dream big, laugh loudly, and remember — whenever you need someone, your birthday twin is only one call away.

WITH ALL MY BEST WISHES, YOUR FRIEND & BIRTHDAY TWIN
— Rabindra
